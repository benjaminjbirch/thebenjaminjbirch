Site information

Privacy

This website does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Age confirmation

The website asks visitors to confirm that they are 18 or older. The answer is not sent to Benjamin J. Birch or stored by this website.

Reader list

Reader-list subscriptions are processed by Buttondown. Your details are used only to send the emails you requested, and every email includes an unsubscribe option.

ARC applications

ARC applications are processed by Formspree and emailed to Benjamin J. Birch. Information submitted through the form is used only to assess and contact potential advance readers.

External links

Retailers, Goodreads, Buttondown, Formspree, and other external websites have their own privacy policies.
